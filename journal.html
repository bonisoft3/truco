2026-10-19 · Edge Runtime Review

Fair deals and durable deadlines

The Cloudflare release uses the shared cluster and machine engine. Review found that UUID version and variant bits entered the deal’s random stream, failed deadlines could block later ones, and an alarm could boot a directory during import. The corrected compiler omits those fixed bits; the edge engine starts fresh rules and the same fuel budget for every step. Resolved deadlines advance their watermark, failed deadlines retry after a bounded delay, and future deadlines keep their alarm. Reset clears alarms before loading data.

The ticker image now includes its shared tick key and CRUD protocol. Rendered documents carry the release’s security policy on fresh and cached responses.

2026-10-19 · Review Follow-up

Quick shouts expire during play

Before, shouting and immediately raising could leave the player’s speech bubble visible indefinitely and suppress later announcements. Gameplay invalidated the revision-bound expiry; a queued move could also consume its timer. Expiry now follows the game and shout identity independently of gameplay. A stale reply stays silent but still schedules expiry, and old timers cannot affect a newer shout.

2026-10-01 · Initial Brief

Truco Paulista & Mineiro (Multiplayer & Boteco Edition)

Truco is the iconic Brazilian card game built for quick, tactical matches. Open it and a table is set: playable with friends over the internet via link (#match/:id) or against boteco regulars. Local-first optimistic mutations with cluster sync (durability: "offline"), syncing game state reactively across tables.

Data

  • Match: Tracks match status, seat assignments (you, partner, opponent1, opponent2), total score (US vs THEM), and game variant.
  • Round: Tracks tricks won (1st, 2nd, 3rd tricks), current phase, active seat, and turn deadlines (ui_deadline, backend_deadline).
  • Held: Tracks 3-card hands per seat and slot index (slot: 0, 1, 2). Opponent cards are masked as face-down backs in the synced state, preserving fog of war against client snooping while keeping slot spatial continuity.
  • Play: Records cards played on the table, which slot they were played from (from_slot), and Truco calls.

Screens & Modes

  • Truco Paulista: Vira rank determines the cyclic Manilhas (Vira + 1). Manilha suits order: 4♣ (Zap) > 7♥ (Copas) > A♠ (Espadilha) > 7♦ (Ouros).
  • Truco Mineiro: Fixed Manilhas (4♣ Zap, 7♥ Copas, A♠ Espadilha, 7♦ Ouros). Truco raises start at 2 points and climb Truco 4 → Seis 8 → Nove 10 → Doze 12.
  • 2-Player (1v1) & 4-Player (2v2) Arena Modes: Play 1v1 or 2v2 across devices via match room links, or with boteco bots (Seu Nezinho, Dona Cida, Tião Pandeiro, Zé da Bicicleta, Bigode) filling vacant seats.
  • Boteco Visuals & Turn Clock: Checked cloth, worn cards, fanned angles, burning rope turn timer, phase tracker, stake badge, and four table themes (Toalha xadrez, Fórmica azul, Neon do bar, Madeira crua).
